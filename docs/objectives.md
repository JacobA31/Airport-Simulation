# Objectives (Report §1–2)

## Primary objective

Determine how check-in and security staffing affects passenger waiting, missed flights, and profit at SFMA.

## Specific objectives

1. Build a DES model of check-in, security, and the gate for commuter and provincial passengers.
2. Quantify baseline performance with 95% confidence intervals for the four principal KPIs, and identify the bottleneck.
3. Compare the baseline with two alternatives within one decision area.
4. Test the recommendation against one uncertain input at low, baseline, and high settings.
5. Recommend an operating policy and state the trade-offs among waiting, missed flights, resource utilization, and cost.
